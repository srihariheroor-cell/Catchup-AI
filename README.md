# ⚡ CatchUp AI — What Did I Miss?
> **Instant Group Chat Intelligence for College Students & Hackathon Teams**  
> Turn 200+ chaotic, unread group messages into urgent alerts, action items with assigned owners, deadlines, and key decisions in seconds.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
College students participate in dozens of high-traffic group chats (WhatsApp project groups, Discord hackathon channels, Slack clubs, and hostel wings). Important announcements, deadline changes, task assignments, and decisions get buried under hundreds of messages, memes, and chatter.

**CatchUp AI** solves this by providing an executive intelligence briefing:
1. **Separates Signal from Noise**: Filters out 70-85% non-essential chatter.
2. **Urgent Alert Center**: Immediately surfaces critical cutoffs, server crashes, and emergency notices with explanatory context.
3. **Action Items & Task Ownership**: Interactive task list with assigned owners, deadlines, and priorities, complete with completion checkboxes and celebratory confetti.
4. **Deadlines & Calendar Sync**: Timeline of scheduled meetings and submission deadlines with 1-click **.ics** export for Google / Apple Calendar.
5. **Key Decisions Log**: Clear record of team consensus so nobody asks "Wait, what did we decide?"
6. **Downloadable Briefing**: Export as Markdown (`.md`), Plain Text (`.txt`), JSON (`.json`), or copy to clipboard.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & AI Architecture
CatchUp AI is designed with strict privacy principles and clear transparency:
- **Default Mode (100% Private Local Heuristic NLP)**:
  - Runs entirely client-side inside the browser using rule-based and heuristic NLP algorithms.
  - Zero external servers, zero database, zero telemetry.
  - No chat data ever leaves your computer.
- **Configured AI Mode (Google Gemini)**:
  - If you enter your own Google Gemini API key in **Settings**, CatchUp AI connects directly from your browser to Google's official Generative Language API (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`).
  - No intermediate proxy servers.
  - API keys are stored solely in your browser's private `localStorage`.
- **Clear Badging**: Every analysis clearly indicates whether it was generated by the Local Heuristic Engine or Live Gemini AI.

## 🧪 How to Test

1. **Test 1-Click Realistic College Scenarios**:
   - Click any of the scenario chips at the top:
     - 🎓 **CS Capstone: ByteForce 404** (Final Moodle submission sprint, AWS bill panic, Prof. Rao demo)
     - 🔥 **Hackathon Squad: Team Neon** (36-hour hack, Gemini API swap, Devpost video deadline)
     - 📢 **Tech Fest: Technova 2026** (Guest speaker flight delay, blown amplifier, sponsor banner crisis)
     - 🏠 **Apartment 302 Chaos** (Landlord inspection notice, Wi-Fi bill, leaking sink)
   - Click the glowing **"Catch Me Up"** button.
   - Inspect the **Executive Intelligence Dossier** and KPI cards.

2. **Test File Upload**:
   - Drag and drop `sample-hackathon-chat.txt` (or click **"Upload .txt File"**).
   - Click **"Catch Me Up"** to analyze the uploaded file.

3. **Test Interactive Tasks**:
   - Switch to the **"Tasks & Action Items"** tab.
   - Check off tasks — watch the progress bar fill up. When the last task is completed, enjoy the celebratory confetti!
   - Click **"+ Add Custom Task"** to add a new action item with custom assignee, due date, and priority.

4. **Test Exports & Calendar**:
   - Click **"Export to Calendar (.ics)"** to download an iCalendar file for Google/Apple Calendar.
   - Click **"Markdown (.md)"**, **"Plain Text (.txt)"**, or **"JSON (.json)"** to download full reports.
   - Click **"Copy Summary"** to test clipboard integration.
   - Click **"Listen (TTS)"** to hear the audio summary read aloud.

5. **Test AI Configuration**:
   - Click **"Configure AI"** in the top right to open Settings.
   - Switch between **Demo (Local Heuristic)** and **Live Gemini AI**.
   - If you have a Google Gemini key, paste it and click **"Test Connection"** to verify live API access.
